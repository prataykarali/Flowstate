## Literature hygiene

- Save paper title, author, venue/year, DOI/arXiv ID, version and exact section.
- Separate **author claims**, **our tests**, and **our hypotheses**.
- Never cite an article as evidence for an experiment we did not run.
- Keep Chapter 7 reading narrow; formal reproduction belongs to Chapter 8.
